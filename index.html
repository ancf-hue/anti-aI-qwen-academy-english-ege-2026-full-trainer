Grammar Traps

Typical mistakes in tasks 19–36 and writing.

Present Perfect vs Past Simple

Present Perfect: action connected to present, no specific time.
Past Simple: action finished, specific time mentioned.

I have visited London three times.
I visited London in 2020.

say / tell / speak / talk

say: without listener (say something).
tell: with listener (tell someone).
speak: language, formal speech.
talk: conversation, chat.

She said she was tired.
He told me the truth.
I speak English and Russian.

Conditionals I / II / III

I: real condition in future.
II: unreal condition in present.
III: unreal condition in past.

If it rains, I will stay home.
If I had time, I would help.
If I had known, I would have come.

Word formation: -ment, -ness, -tion, -ity, -ful, -less, -able

Suffixes change part of speech. In tasks 25–29 identify required part of speech.

develop → development
happy → happiness
care → careful / careless

Phrasal verbs

Often checked in tasks 30–36:

give up — quit
pick up — collect
put off — postpone
look after — care for

Passive Voice

Formed via be + V3. Tense shown by be.

Tea is drunk to music.
It was used for medicine.
The museum was founded in 1755.

Reported Speech

Tense usually shifts back when reporting.

He said, “I am tired.”
He said that he was tired.

She said, “I have finished.”
She said that she had finished.
